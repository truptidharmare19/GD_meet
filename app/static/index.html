Speech recognition runs in each participant's browser and may be processed by the browser provider. When you end the meeting, captions are appended to transcript.txt in the project folder and the transcript is sent to Google Gemini for feedback. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

Allow camera and microphone access to join the live discussion. Speech transcription works in supported browsers such as Chrome and Edge.
